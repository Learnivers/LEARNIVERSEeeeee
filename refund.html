We want every family to be happy with their purchase. If something goes wrong, we will make it right.

1. Our Promise

If you have a problem with a purchase from Learniverse, contact us first. Most issues can be fixed quickly by re-sending your book, correcting your order, or giving a refund.

2. Digital Books

Digital books are delivered right after payment, and they cannot be “returned” once opened. For this reason, we generally do not give refunds for digital books that have been opened or downloaded, except in the cases listed below.

3. When You Can Get a Refund

You may request a refund within [7] days of purchase if:

  • you were charged twice for the same book (duplicate purchase);
  • the book file is damaged, incomplete, or will not open, and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time;
  • you received a different book from the one you ordered;
  • the purchase was made without your permission and you report it promptly;
  • the book is significantly different from its description.

4. When We Cannot Refund

  • You changed your mind after opening or downloading a digital book.
  • The request is made after [7] days, unless the law gives you a longer period.
  • The book was shared, copied, or used in a way that breaks our Terms & Conditions.

5. Printed & Personalized Books (if offered)

If we offer printed books, you may return or replace a book that arrives damaged, defective, or wrong. Please send clear photos within [7] days of delivery. Because personalized books (with a child’s name, photo, or message) are made just for you, we cannot accept returns for them unless they arrive damaged, defective, or with our printing mistake.

6. How to Request a Refund

Email [your.email@example.com] with:

  • your order number and the email you used to buy;
  • the title of the book;
  • a short explanation of the problem, with screenshots or photos if possible.

We will reply within [3] business days.

7. How Refunds Are Paid

Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method (for example, e-wallet, bank, or card) within [5–10] business days. Your bank or payment provider may need extra time to show it. Access to a refunded book will be removed from your account.

8. Your Legal Rights

This policy does not take away your rights as an online consumer under Philippine law, including the Internet Transactions Act of 2023 (Republic Act No. 11967) and the Consumer Act of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 7394). These laws allow remedies such as repair, replacement, or refund in cases like defective goods or services. You may also bring a complaint to the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI).
