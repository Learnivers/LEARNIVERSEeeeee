What is Learniverse?

Learniverse is a digital book platform designed to make learning fun, enjoyable, and accessible for children and young learners. It provides educational books in different learning areas, including Language and Reading, Mathematics and Logic, Science and Discovery, Arts and Creativity, and Life Skills and Values. Through Learniverse, children can explore new ideas, develop their reading skills, gain knowledge, and discover their creativity while enjoying an interactive learning experience.

Parents and guardians can also use Learniverse to find reliable learning materials that support their children’s growth and continued learning at home.

Five Learning Areas

Every book in Learniverse belongs to one of five learning areas.

📚

Language & Reading

Alphabet, vocabulary, grammar, Filipino language, and reading comprehension.

🔢

Mathematics & Logic

Numbers, shapes, puzzles, critical thinking, and money smarts.

🔬

Science & Discovery

Animals, nature, space, dinosaurs, and technology.

🎨

Arts & Creativity

Drawing, music, and cooking for kids.

💛

Life Skills & Values

Good manners, health, feelings, safety, and community helpers.
